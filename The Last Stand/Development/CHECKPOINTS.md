# Checkpoint tracker

Status key: **In progress**, **Not started**, **Complete**. Checkpoints and
their order are adjustable; confirm scope at the start of each one.

## Checkpoint 0 — Project foundation

**Status:** In progress

**Goal:** Capture the product vision, working principles, flexible roadmap,
and lightweight progress workflow without committing to a technology stack or
implementing game features.

**Acceptance goals:**

- The README explains that fully playable chess comes first.
- Battle framing and presentation are optional layers that do not interfere
  with chess.
- The roadmap and development record have a clear home.
- The technology choice remains open until a recommendation is based on the
  workspace and project needs.

**Next action:** Review this foundation, then recommend a suitable technology
stack after inspecting any relevant project constraints. No implementation
work begins until that recommendation is discussed.

## Checkpoint 1 — The chessboard

**Status:** Not started

**Goal:** Establish the board, pieces, movement, and basic interaction. Define
and review the implementation approach after the stack decision.

## Checkpoint 2 — Chess rules

**Status:** Not started

**Goal:** Support legal moves and the complete rules needed for standard chess,
including check, checkmate, castling, and promotion.

## Checkpoint 3 — First playable match

**Status:** Not started

**Goal:** Make a complete match playable with clear controls and a usable core
interface.

## Checkpoint 4 — Character reactions

**Status:** Not started

**Goal:** Explore optional, contextual reactions and dialogue that leave the
board and move flow clear.

## Checkpoint 5 — Battlefield and roles

**Status:** Not started

**Goal:** Explore optional attacker/defender framing, battlefields, and
cinematic introductions around a match.

## Checkpoint 6 — Presentation

**Status:** Not started

**Goal:** Explore animation, sound, effects, and storytelling while preserving
uninterrupted chess play.

## Checkpoint 7 — Expanded experience

**Status:** Not started

**Goal:** Consider opponent options, settings, and additional game modes based
on what has been learned from the playable game.
